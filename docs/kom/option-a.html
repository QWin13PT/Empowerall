Four places with a long memory.

Option A: merge sections 04 (Living Labs) and 05 (Long-term observation) into one section. This page is a discussion sketch. It is not on the live site.

EMPOWERALL develops four Agroecology Living Labs in Spain, France, Romania and Slovakia. Each is embedded in a Long-Term Socio-Ecological Research (LTSER) Platform of eLTER RI, the European research infrastructure that has observed these landscapes and their communities for years. The Living Labs bring new generation farmers and local actors together to co-create agroecological change; the Platforms provide the data, monitoring and research networks to follow it over time and share it across Europe.
